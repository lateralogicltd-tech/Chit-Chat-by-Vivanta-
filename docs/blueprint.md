# **App Name**: History Canvas

## Core Features:

- Era Selector: Allows Vivanta to choose a historical period (e.g., Ancient Egypt, Roman Empire, Medieval Knights) as the setting for their creation.
- AI Character Builder: A tool that suggests historical character profiles (e.g., appearance, role, simple personality traits) relevant to the chosen era based on Vivanta's initial ideas.
- Scene Idea Input: Provides an input field for Vivanta to describe a simple scene, object, or concept they want to explore within the selected historical period.
- AI Scene Generator: A tool that uses the chosen era, character ideas, and Vivanta's input to craft short, descriptive historical scene summaries or story prompts.
- Creation Display: Presents the generated characters and scene descriptions in an easy-to-read and visually appealing format, possibly with simple thematic background visuals.
- Saved Creations Library: Allows Vivanta to save their favorite generated characters and scene descriptions to review, edit, or get new ideas from later.

## Style Guidelines:

- The visual design will adopt a light color scheme, reflecting clarity, child-friendliness, and a sense of new discovery. The main hue, informed by historical documents and ancient artifacts, will be a warm, golden yellow. The primary interactive color will be a slightly muted golden yellow (#DBA44F), embodying an inviting and active discovery process. The background will be a soft, light cream (#FAF4E6), visibly of the same golden hue but heavily desaturated to provide a calm canvas for creations. An accent color, a vivid orange-red (#E54C1A), will be used to draw attention to interactive elements and highlight new creations, offering a dynamic contrast.
- The font for all text, including headlines and body, will be 'PT Sans' (humanist sans-serif). Its modern yet warm aesthetic is suitable for both educational content and creative prompts for an 8-year-old, ensuring excellent readability and an engaging feel.
- Icons will be simple, friendly, and illustrative, reflecting historical elements and creative actions. Use clear, easily recognizable silhouettes for actions like 'save', 'generate', and 'select era'.
- A clean, card-based layout will organize information and generated content. Ample whitespace will ensure an uncluttered interface, making it easy for young users to navigate and focus on their creative tasks.
- Subtle, smooth transition animations will be used when navigating between eras or displaying new generated content, adding a touch of magic and responsiveness without distracting from the creative flow.
